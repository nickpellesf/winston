# Winston privacy policy

Winston is used only by the household that runs it. It has no other users.

When the owner connects their Google Nest account, Winston receives doorbell press notifications so it can announce them on the home's speakers. The sign-in token is stored only on the household's own Raspberry Pi.

No data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. Nothing is kept beyond what is needed to make the announcement.

The owner can disconnect Winston at any time from their Google account's connected apps page.
